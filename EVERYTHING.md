## 4 · HELP-FIRST OUTREACH (find complaints → answer them)

**Three rules:** help first, fully (link only if it directly solves the ask) ·
signal-based, never mass (reply to a specific fresh post) · one channel per week.

**Hunting grounds:** Reddit r/smallbusiness + r/Entrepreneur ("drowning",
"chasing invoices", "tried ChatGPT") — 3 deep answers/week · Facebook local
business groups — 5 helpful answers before ANY link · Google Maps reviews (warm
research) · X search "need help with" + niche — reply in 30 min · YouTube comments
under business channels — answer, no link unless asked.

**Five paste-ready responses** (each answers the real drain first):

- **"Everything goes through me / midnight work"** → That feeling has a name —
  skills waste: the most expensive person doing $15/hr work. Write down everything
  you did last week, mark what someone else could do with a one-page instruction,
  hand off the bottom of the list first. ("Fire yourself from the bottom up.")
  Free 10-minute time audit does exactly this scoring — happy to link if useful.
- **"Constantly chasing invoices/leads"** → The chase happens because it lives in
  your head. A drafted-followup ladder (day 0 clear terms → day 7 friendly →
  day 14 firm → day 21 pause work) removes the remembering; you just approve
  sends. Unpaid invoices are money you already earned. Template's free if you want it.
- **"Revenue up but I'm broke/stressed"** → Most of that stress is not knowing the
  number — owners lose 33 workdays/yr to financial worry, mostly from avoiding the
  number. A 13-week cash forecast takes ~10 minutes and turns dread into a
  decision. Free calculator exists; link on request.
- **"ChatGPT gave me generic garbage"** → Generic in, generic out. A bare question
  gets an intern's guess; a role card — identity, your context, the job, 3
  examples of your best work — gets a senior draft in your voice. Free module on
  the format if you want it.
- **"I don't know what my real problem is"** → In a 2026 study of 100 owner posts,
  1 in 3 named the wrong problem — strangers in the comments spotted the real one
  because they weren't standing inside the business. Write the 2–3 things you'd
  post about, then ask what a commenter who could see your books and inbox would
  say is behind each.

**Never:** unsolicited DMs at scale, link-drop first comments, hidden affiliation
(if asked, we say we built the academy), fake accounts.

**Weekly loop:** Mon pick the ground → Wed 3 full answers → Fri log which drains
appeared most → those become next week's content topics.
