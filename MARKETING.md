# 📣 Launch & Distribution Kit — YouTube + Facebook

Everything needed to take the academy's videos public. **Account creation must be
done by a human** (Google/Meta require identity, phone and login) — that's a
20-minute one-time setup below; after that, this kit makes every upload a
copy-paste job, and a Zapier connection can automate posting entirely.

## One-time setup (you, ~20 min)

1. **YouTube**: youtube.com → profile → "Create a channel" → name:
   `AI Business Academy` · handle: `@AIBusinessAcademyHQ` (or nearest free).
   Channel art: generate with the Nano Banana 2 identity block in PRODUCTION.md.
   About text: first paragraph of README.md + site link.
2. **Facebook Page**: facebook.com/pages/create → Business → same name/art.
3. **Automation (optional but recommended)**: connect YouTube + Facebook Pages to
   your Zapier account, then Claude can push posts/uploads through the Zapier
   connector in future sessions.

## Networking playbook

- Comment as the Page on 5 SMB/AI creators' posts daily (add value, never pitch).
- Offer the ROI Reel + chart free to 3 local business groups/newsletters per week.
- Pitch: "free 17-min film + DNA scan for your members — no signup, no catch."

## Measurement (weekly, 10 min)

Views → click-throughs to the site (add `?src=yt` / `?src=fb` to links) → DNA scans
started. Log in a sheet; double down on whichever post style drove scans.
