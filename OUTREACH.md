# 🤝 OUTREACH.md — the help-first complaint-response playbook

How we "find people's needs and complaints and let them know how we can help"
— without ever becoming spam. Built from the 2026 founder-marketing research:
broad broadcasting wastes attention; what converts is going **narrow and deep
where people already have intent and pain**.

## The three rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Help first, fully.** Answer the person's actual question with a complete,
   usable answer. Mention the academy only when it directly solves the thing
   they asked — or when they ask. One high-quality reply beats ten drive-bys.
2. **Signal-based, never mass.** Reply to a *specific person's specific post*
   within hours of it appearing. Generic cold blasts have collapsing reply
   rates; catching someone right after they voice the frustration converts.
3. **One channel done well beats five done halfway.** Pick one hunting ground
   per week. Burnout and shadow-bans come from spreading thin.

## Where the complaints live (hunting grounds)

|---|---|---|
| Reddit r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, trade subs | "drowning", "overwhelmed", "chasing invoices", "leads go cold", "tried ChatGPT" | 3 deep answers/week |

## The complaint → response map (paste-ready templates)

Each template answers the *underlying* drain (from the 100-post taxonomy),
gives a complete free fix, and only then mentions the matching free tool.

**"Everything goes through me / I work until midnight"** (skills waste, 80%)
> That feeling has a name — skills waste: the most expensive person in the
> company doing $15/hr work. What worked for us: write down everything you did
> last week, mark what someone else could do with a one-page instruction, and
> hand off the bottom of the list first. ("Fire yourself from the bottom up.")
> If you want it structured, there's a free 10-minute time audit that does
> exactly this scoring — happy to link it if useful.

**"I'm constantly chasing invoices / leads / people"** (follow-up chase, 22%)
> The chase only happens because it lives in your head. A drafted-followup
> ladder (day 0 clear terms → day 7 friendly → day 14 firm → day 21 pause work)
> removes the remembering; you just approve sends. Unpaid invoices are money
> you already earned — worth 20 minutes to set up. Template's free if you want it.

**"Revenue is up but I'm broke / stressed about money"** (cash visibility)
> Most of that stress is not knowing the number — 2026 survey: owners lose 33
> workdays/yr to financial worry, and most of it comes from avoiding the number,
> not from what it says. A 13-week cash forecast takes ~10 minutes to build and
> turns dread into a decision. Free calculator exists; link on request.

**"I tried ChatGPT and it was generic garbage"** (role-card gap)
> Generic in, generic out. A bare question gets an intern's guess; a role card
> — identity, your context, the job, 3 examples of your best work — gets a
> senior draft in your voice. That one change is the difference between "using
> AI" and it actually working. There's a free module on it if you want the format.

**"I don't even know what my real problem is"** (misdiagnosis, 30%)
> In a 2026 study of 100 owner posts, 1 in 3 owners named the wrong problem —
> and strangers in the comments spotted the real one, because they weren't
> standing inside the business. Quick outside-view trick: write the 2–3 things
> you'd post about, then ask what a commenter who could see your books and
> inbox would say is behind each. If the answer involves work you personally
> shouldn't be doing, or a step that exists only in your head — that's the target.

## What we never do

- No unsolicited DMs at scale. No link-dropping as a first comment.
- No pretending to be a disinterested bystander — if asked, we say we built
  the academy. Disclosure is a trust advantage (Ethica's standard).
- No engagement-bait or fake accounts. One real voice, consistently helpful.
- Posting FROM accounts requires the owner's connected accounts (Zapier once
  YouTube/Facebook are linked) — this playbook is drafted for the owner to send.

## The weekly loop

Mon: pick the week's ground → Wed: 3 full help-first answers → Fri: log which
drains you saw most (feed Census) → those become next week's content topics.
Complaints are blueprints; this file is the pipe from blueprint to content.
