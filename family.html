FIELD MANUAL

How to hire your first agent (5 minutes)

  1. 1

    Copy the hire prompt of the agent matching your biggest leak (your DNA scan and Time Audit name it).

  2. 2

    Paste it into any capable AI assistant and add 3 lines of context: what you sell, who buys it, how your voice sounds.

  3. 3

    Test on solved work first — last month's report, an email you already answered well. Grade it against your version.

  4. 4

    Add your standards — paste 2–3 examples of work you consider excellent. Agents learn taste from examples, not adjectives.

  5. 5

    Go live with a review gate — nothing ships without your eye for the first month. Autonomy is earned in inches.
