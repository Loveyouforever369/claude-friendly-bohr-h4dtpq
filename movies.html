HOW TO WATCH

Get the cinema experience

🎧

Headphones on

Movies narrate in a deeper, slower voice than the lessons. Tune voice & speed in the Audio Command Deck once a film is playing.

📖

Chapters & scenes

The chapter name shows under the screen. Click any progress segment to jump; ←/→ steps scenes; Space pauses.

🎭

Watch for cast cards

When agents enter the story, their cards appear on screen — name, role, superpower. Every one is hireable in the AI Family.

🍿

Cinema mode

The 🍿 button in the player auto-plays the next film with a countdown — the feature, the chronicles and the character films run back-to-back like a series binge.

🎯

Then run the play

Each film ends pointing at the exact tool Sam used — the scan, the audit, the sprint. Watching is step zero — and every film's full script downloads as a spreadsheet.
