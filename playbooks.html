THE INSTALL

The 14-day deployment path

1️⃣

Days 1–2 · Baseline

Pick one playbook. Record the before-numbers: hours in email, response time, posts shipped, whatever its scoreboard names. No baseline, no bragging rights.

2️⃣

Days 3–5 · Hire the crew

Copy the hire prompts from the AI Family. Feed each agent your real context. Everything they produce is a draft in a folder — nothing customer-facing yet.

3️⃣

Days 6–8 · Approval rhythm

Two 15-minute review blocks a day: approve, edit, or kill what the agents drafted. Judgment stays yours; typing stops being your job.

4️⃣

Days 9–12 · Measure & tighten

Compare against the baseline. Tighten the prompts that miss your voice. Kill anything you rubber-stamp without reading — that's automation debt.

5️⃣

Days 13–14 · Compound

Deepen this playbook or add a second. The second install is twice as fast — the approval rhythm already exists.

🛡️

The standing rule

Agents draft. You approve. Nothing sends itself. Money, messages and public posts always pass a human. This rule is the product.
