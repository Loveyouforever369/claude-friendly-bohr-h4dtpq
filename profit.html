GROWTH ENGINE · FIVE LEVERS

The Profit Lab

Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is oxygen. These five AI levers surface the profit already hiding in your business — most owners find 8–20% in the first month, no new customers required.

🏷️

1 · Pricing intelligence

Agent: Meter. Value-based price review + the math of a 2–5% test. Most owners undercharge 15–30% on their best work.

🩸

2 · Leak hunting

Agents: Hawk, Toll. Expense autopsy + receivables chase. Typical find: 3–8% of revenue leaking through holes too small to see.

📐

3 · Margin mix

Agent: Margin. Profit-per-hour ranking of every offer — including your time. Scale the quiet winner, fix or kill the prestige loser.

💧

4 · Cash forecasting

Agents: Stream, Almanac. 13-week forecast updated Fridays + seasonality prep. Crunches announce themselves two months early.

🗝️

5 · Evidence-based offers

Agents: Mosaic, Alchemist, Probe. Mine feedback for what customers keep asking, repackage, validate in 7 days before building.

PLAYBOOKS BY BUSINESS TYPE

Your first three profit moves

🛠 Service businesses (agencies, trades, consultants) ▸

Move 1 — Same-day proposals

Bridge turns call notes into 3-option proposals. Speed-to-quote is the #1 close-rate lever in services.

Move 2 — Profit-per-hour ranking

Margin exposes which service actually pays. Most firms find a 4–6× spread between their best and worst offer.

Move 3 — Productize the repeatable

Alchemist converts your most repeated custom work into a fixed-scope, fixed-price product with margin you control.

🛍 E-commerce & retail ▸

Move 1 — Cart & browse recovery

Cartwright's 3-touch sequence typically recovers 10–15% of abandoned carts — the cheapest revenue in your business.

Move 2 — AOV engineering

Gondola designs bundles and cross-sell maps; a 15% AOV lift beats most ad campaigns at zero acquisition cost.

Move 3 — Listing optimization

Storefront rewrites titles and bullets for both human buyers and the AI shopping agents that increasingly buy on their behalf.

📍 Local & brick-and-mortar ▸

Move 1 — Kill the missed call

A disclosed voice/chat assistant catches the ~40% of calls small businesses miss. Each one is a booking walking to a rival.

Move 2 — Review velocity

Ripple triggers asks at peak-happiness moments; Halo answers everything in 24h. Local rank follows review velocity.

Move 3 — Own the AI answer

Maple + Lumen make you the business AI assistants cite when someone asks "best ___ near me."

💻 SaaS, creators & digital ▸

Move 1 — Churn saves

Sentry's warning signals + save plays. Recovering 2 of 10 churning customers usually outearns 20% more traffic.

Move 2 — Content compounding

The Quill→Remix→Echo engine ships a week of assets from 15 minutes of your truth. Consistency is the algorithm hack.

Move 3 — Pricing & packaging test

Meter + Splitter design a clean test of a higher tier. Digital margins make pricing the most leveraged number you own.
